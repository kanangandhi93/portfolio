CRITICAL (CVSS 9.3) PKI & Cryptographic Architecture Target: Multinational Retail & Fashion Conglomerate

Public Exposure of Internal PKI Trust Chain via JWKS x5c

Scope: GET /oauth2/realms/root/connect/jwk_uri
Status: Remediated & Verified

// 01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A public OpenAM JWKS endpoint leaked full X.509 certificate chains in the `x5c` parameter. Certificate extensions pointed to internal Certificate Authority URLs that resolved publicly on the internet, allowing unauthenticated public download of the Root CA, Sub CA, 15KB Certificate Revocation List (CRL), and active OCSP responder.

// 02. PROOF OF CONCEPT & REPRODUCTION FLOW

### 1. Certificate Extraction via JWKS Extracted CDP (CRL Distribution Points) and AIA (Authority Information Access) extensions from public JWKS: ```bash GET /openam/oauth2/realms/root/realms/itxid/connect/jwk_uri ``` ### 2. Public Download of CA Infrastructure Artifacts ```bash GET /CertData/Root_CA.crt -> HTTP 200 OK (1,519 bytes, DER-encoded ASN.1 SEQUENCE) GET /CertData/Corporate_SubCA.crt -> HTTP 200 OK (1,335 bytes, DER-encoded ASN.1 SEQUENCE) GET /CertData/Corporate_SubCA.crl -> HTTP 200 OK (15,494 bytes, full active revocation list) POST /ocsp (application/ocsp-request) -> HTTP 200 OK (OCSP responder active and queryable) ``` ### 3. Internal Hostname Reconnaissance X.509 SANs revealed internal hostnames (`*.central.[redacted].grp`), internal load balancers, and JWT signing key aliases, mapping corporate internal network topology.

// 03. BUSINESS IMPACT & REGULATORY EXPOSURE

Complete exposure of enterprise internal trust hierarchy, enabling internal network TLS interception, service mapping, and potential MiTM attacks when chained with SSRF.

// 04. REMEDIATION & QA ENGINEERING GUIDANCE

1. **Remove x5c from JWKS:** Cease exposing full X.509 certificate chains in public JWKS endpoints. Output only the standard public key parameters (kty, kid, use, n, e). 2. **Restrict PKI Access to Internal Networks:** Move internal PKI distribution points and OCSP responders behind internal enterprise VPNs/firewalls. 3. **CA Rotation & Revocation:** Rotate exposed intermediate CA certificates and revoke compromised keys.
